# Changelog

## 1.0.1
- Add Esperanto and Latin stat labels and recognize the Ukrainian `uk` alias.
- Distinguish Korean fire and burn labels when switching languages.
- Clarify that P.E.L.E is optional.
- Updated minimum dependencies to SandsweptTeam-Sandswept 1.4.6 and DropPod-LookingGlass 1.16.3.
- Covers known Sandswept item and equipment stat labels supplied to LookingGlass.
- Added localized label maps and a language-change handler; in-game verification remains pending.

## 1.0.0
- Initial release.
- Localizes Sandswept item and equipment stat labels added by LookingGlass.
- Supports EN, PT-BR, DE, ES, FR, IT, JA, KO, PL, RU, TR, UA, ZH-CN, and ZH-TW.
- Keeps the normal Sandswept language files untouched.
