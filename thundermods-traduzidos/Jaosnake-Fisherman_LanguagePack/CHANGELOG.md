# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- Initial release of the Fisherman translation pack for Risk of Rain 2.
- Full multi-language support across 15 canonical languages: English (`en`), German (`de`), Spanish LATAM (`es-419`), Spanish Spain (`es-ES`), French (`FR`), Italian (`IT`), Japanese (`ja`), Korean (`ko`), Latin (`la`), Brazilian Portuguese (`pt-BR`), Russian (`RU`), Turkish (`tr`), Ukrainian (`UA`), Simplified Chinese (`zh-CN`), and Esperanto (`eo`).
- 100% of tokens translated (35/35 per language) strictly matching original DLL tokens:
  - Survivor name, subtitle ("Sickly Poacher"), and complete survival tips.
  - Official brig incident report log from UES Safe Travels.
  - Outro victory flavor and failure lost at sea messages.
  - Complete ability suite: Debone (Primary), Cast (Secondary), F153 Mobile Shanty Platform & Strange Friend (Utility), Man o' War & Steady the Nerves (Special), and The Hooks! (Passive).
  - Minion tags: Junior and Fisherman's Friend.
  - Keywords: Treasure, Non-Lethal, Tether, Unfinished, Regenerative, Smackable, Soulbound, Directable.
  - Mastery skin and Mastery achievement ("Fisherman: Mastery").
- Dynamic placeholders `{0}` and `{1}` rigorously preserved.
- 100% TextMeshPro tag balance verified with zero syntax or color leakage.
- Strict UTF-8 encoding without BOM.
