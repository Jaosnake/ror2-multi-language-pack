# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- Initial release of the RealerMysticsItems multi-language translation pack targeting `Phreel-RealerMysticsItems-2.1.32`.
- Complete coverage across all 15 supported languages (333 tokens per language):
  - English (`en`), German (`de`), Spanish LATAM (`es-419`), Spanish Spain (`es-ES`), French (`FR`), Italian (`IT`), Japanese (`ja`), Korean (`ko`), Brazilian Portuguese (`pt-BR`), Russian (`RU`), Turkish (`tr`), Ukrainian (`UA`), Simplified Chinese (`zh-CN`), Esperanto (`eo`), and Latin (`la`).
- Full pt-BR revision: re-translated from European Portuguese into natural Brazilian Portuguese with 100% official RoR2 terminology.
- 100% preservation of all TextMeshPro tags and dynamic string formatting parameters (`{ChanceBonus}`, `{ArmorAdd}`, `{FlatHealing}`, `{Radius}`, `{Time}`, etc.).
- Full P.E.L.E support for Ukrainian, Esperanto, and Latin.
