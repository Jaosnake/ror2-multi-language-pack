## 🎮 Included Translation
- **Survivor Identity**: Gunslinger ("Peacekeeper"), overview, description, and lore.
- **Victory & Defeat Outros**: Outro flavor ("..singing Take Me Home") and void defeat lines.
- **Passive & Keywords**: Wraith Form passive and Hover keywords.
- **Complete Combat Kit**:
  - **Primary**: Vendetta (with reload mechanics and 6th bullet crit)
  - **Alt Primary / Secondary**: Unload (with stacking crit/damage mechanics)
  - **Secondary**: Accursed Arm & Accursed Orb
  - **Utility**: Wraith Step & Wraith Frenzy
  - **Special**: Soul Piercer & Deadeye (both targeting and hovering forms)
- **Custom Mod Items**:
  - **Wicked Ring**: Name, pickup, full stats description, and item lore.
  - **Infinity Gauntlet**: Name, pickup, full description, and lore quote.
- **All 12 In-game Achievements & Unlockables**:

  - Gunslinger: Mastery (`GUNSLINGER_MASTERY_*`)
  - Gunslinger: Elite Mastery (`GUNSLINGER_ELITE_*`)
  - Gunslinger: I Kill With My Heart (`GUNSLINGER_DEADEYE_*`)
  - Gunslinger: BFG On His Hip (`GUNSLINGER_PREON_*`)
  - Gunslinger: Dead Man Walkin' (`GUNSLINGER_DEATHMARK_*`)
  - Gunslinger: End Of The Line (`GUNSLINGER_UNLOAD_*`)
  - Gunslinger: Electric Organs (`GUNSLINGER_LORD_*`)
  - Gunslinger: Wicked Entity (`GUNSLINGER_WICKED_*`)
  - Gunslinger: Vasto Lorde (`GUNSLINGER_HOLLOW_*`)
  - Salvation (`GUNSLINGER_GAUNTLET_*`)
  - Gunslinger: Lucky 7 (`GUNSLINGER_GAMBLE_*`)

## 📥 Installation
1. Install [Thunderstore Mod Manager](https://www.overwolf.com/app/Thunderstore-Thunderstore_Mod_Manager) or [r2modman](https://github.com/ebkr/r2modmanPlus).
2. Install **Dragonyck-Gunslinger** and **R2API_Language**.
3. Install **Jaosnake-Gunslinger_LanguagePack**.
4. Launch the game in your preferred language!
